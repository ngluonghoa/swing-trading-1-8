1. ĐẦU VÀO / PHÂN TẦNG CỦA EARLY DETECTION


Inputs minimum: project/version, symbol/exchange, decision_time (UTC+07), current market session and calendar version, last confirmed M15/H1/D1/W1, M15 structure/pivots/support-resistance, candidate WatchZones with zone_id + revision + timeframe + expiry timestamp, last traded price and timestamp with trusted price_basis, ATR D1 computed from compatible series, position_context, source quality and provisional source snapshot. Optional features: M5 stream/history (only if armed), same-slot RVOL history, matched trade value/VWAP, market/sector state.
M15 baseline ALWAYS under analysis engine scope when available; closed M15 can support causal HH/HL/BOS/CHoCH, trend, support/rejection and candidate watch; still flag missing data. Prior L3/L4 watch zones from last run may be reused VALIDATED. Cold start: coarse watch candidates are constructed from D1/H1/M15 structure without requiring M5 so no cyclic dependency.
M5 adds micro price action only when ARMED; independent 16 detector registry evaluation MUST NOT be skipped when OFF. If required TF/trigger unknown, corresponding zone output UNVERIFIED/NOT_DETECTED according to actual dependencies (NOT_DETECTED only if enough higher-TF negative evidence). B1/S1 major zone definitions must not be based only on M5. Output carries source revisions and per-TF closed status.


2. STATE MACHINE VÀ CÁC NHÁNH ƯU TIÊN


Proposed M5 mode states: IDLE, ARMED, ARMED_PROTECTION, COOLDOWN, EXPIRED; quality VALID/STALE/UNVERIFIED/BLOCKED is ORTHOGONAL, not a false zone state. Each state is per symbol + zone_id + zone_revision + session/calendar_version + strategy/position scope, versioned in cache, with explicit transition event timestamp.
PRIORITY 1 SOURCE VALIDITY: verify trusted price/source, compatible D1 ATR/price basis, clock and calendar. If missing/STALE, do not create confirmed M5 trigger; retain state for inspection with quality UNVERIFIED and reason. No new zone arm based on untrusted price. A current protective position still requires safety review/fallback based on reliable higher-TF information and position; never invent a verified exit.
PRIORITY 2 POSITION PROTECTION — OPEN_VERIFIED PositionContext với verified risk/protection reason → ARMED_PROTECTION (ưu tiên cả khi BUY zone far/EXPIRED/cooldown unapproved). Step04 kiểm vị thế và source/as_of; NOT_PROVIDED→NO_POSITION_CHECKED không có nghĩa flat, STALE/CONFLICTING→POSITION_UNVERIFIED + warning/BLOCK position-dependent action, không giả được bảo vệ khi thiếu source. Priority 2 độc lập trạng thái ARM cho BUY zone và không đặt lệnh broker.
PRIORITY 3 ZONE EXPIRY / STRUCTURAL INVALIDATION — NORMATIVE v0.3: Khi có sự kiện vô hiệu hóa cấu trúc VERIFIED (price basis/session/as-of phù hợp), đánh dấu zone_revision hiện tại INVALIDATED và state=EXPIRED ngay, kèm event_id. Riêng điều kiện decision_at >= zoneExpiresAt CHỈ được chuyển EXPIRED nếu clock_policy_version (WALL_CLOCK hoặc TRADING_ACTIVE_MINUTES), lịch sàn, phiên và nguồn thời gian đã APPROVED + TESTED, threshold TTL đã được phê duyệt. Nếu clock/TTL còn UNAPPROVED, không chuyển EXPIRED vì đồng hồ; ghi time_expiry_eligibility=UNVERIFIED, tiếp tục kiểm tra structural safety/position và không phát hành confirmed time-based decision. EXPIRED của revision đã bị vô hiệu hóa là terminal; chỉ zone_id/revision MỚI có sự kiện candidate VERIFIED độc lập được khởi tạo scope mới ở IDLE, không hồi sinh cùng revision vì giá lại gần zone. Ưu tiên ARMED_PROTECTION đối với OPEN_VERIFIED position không bị tắt vì zone EXPIRED.
PRIORITY 4 NEAR-ZONE / M15 EARLY: validated quote-to-zone distance<=armThreshold×verified ATR_D1 hoặc M15 CLOSED+VERIFIED candidate event có thể tạo ARMED từ IDLE qua T-01, subject to approved source/zone eligibility. Một provisional/open M15 hoặc provisional quote chỉ có thể phát bounded FETCH_PREVIEW_REQUEST với quality tag và NO CONFIRMED ARM/TRADE, không tạo state transition/duplicate semantic arm alert; chỉ chuyển chính thức khi evidence validated và clock/candidate gate hợp lệ. COOLDOWN/EXPIRED không bypass T-01 limitations qua provisional price.
PRIORITY 5 HYSTERESIS — QUY TẮC HIỆN HÀNH (v1.1): Giữ ARMED giữa armThreshold và disarmThreshold. Chỉ chuyển COOLDOWN khi giá xa zone > disarmThreshold trên HAI bar M15 KHÁC NHAU, LIÊN TIẾP THEO ORDINAL CỦA LỊCH SÀN, ĐÃ CLOSED+VERIFIED và đã qua minHold hợp lệ. Mỗi M15MarketBarId=MarketBarKey (05 §2; định nghĩa đầy đủ ở 06 §15.1) được xử lý đúng MỘT LẦN cho cùng state_version; người dùng quét M5 nhiều lần trong cùng bar M15 không tăng farCount. Identity của bar không chứa source_revision; revision mới của bar đã đánh giá phải yêu cầu deterministic replay từ checkpoint, không tăng như bar mới. Khi gặp một M15 CLOSED nhưng thiếu nguồn hay sparse/unverified, chuỗi verified-consecutive bị ngắt và phải ghi reason; open bar không được đếm. Nếu bar đã đánh giá xuất hiện lại không đổi thì idempotent. Nếu nến tới sai thứ tự hoặc thiếu ordinal, không xác nhận tính liên tiếp khi chưa đối soát lịch; fallback safe hold/UNVERIFIED. Reset farCount khi giá quay gần zone theo dữ liệu đủ chất lượng. Nếu minHold/clock policy chưa được kiểm chứng thì hai nến far có thể được ghi nhận làm bằng chứng nhưng KHÔNG tự chuyển COOLDOWN; COOLDOWN expiry chỉ được xác nhận theo phiên bản clock policy đã nghiệm thu; rearm đòi live zone/candidate hợp lệ.
PRIORITY 6 MARKET CLOSED: new M5 history fetch should stop/TTL evolve based on exchange calendar. Source revision/event id changes invalidate previous micro evidence, with no duplicate early alert event for same confirmed event_id.
System must distinguish active watch state vs M5 confirmed trigger. ARMED does not mean DETECTED; DETECTED is only a detector outcome with its own evidence.


3. PARAMETER REGISTRY B4 v0.1 — ĐỀ XUẤT CHƯA BACKTEST


P03 distance armThreshold = 0.40 x ATR(D1) compatible price basis, computed to nearest zone boundary; use max(0, gap) and avoid averaging price percent with ATR units.
P16 disarmThreshold = 0.70 x ATR(D1) (larger than arm to prevent flapping).
P16 minHold = 15 phút là CANDIDATE chưa duyệt; chỉ áp dụng khi clock_policy_version được nghiệm thu cùng lịch sàn, không mặc định dùng elapsed wall time.
P16 disarm requires 2 consecutive CLOSED M15 bars beyond 0.70 ATR.
P16 cooldown = 10 phút là CANDIDATE chưa duyệt; COOLDOWN không được rearm tự động vì giá gần vùng; cần approved clock + verified same-revision live candidate, hoặc tạo zone_revision mới từ verified structural event. OPEN_VERIFIED position protection luôn được xử lý độc lập.
P16 CANDIDATE zoneTTL = 45 phút là THAM SỐ THỬ NGHIỆM CHƯA BACKTEST, KHÔNG phải quy tắc đã được duyệt để xác nhận hoặc hết hạn tự động. TTL/hold/cooldown sử dụng TRADING_ACTIVE_MINUTES hay WALL_CLOCK hiện CHƯA CHỐT: phải versioned per-exchange calendar và được kiểm thử qua nghỉ trưa, ATO/ATC/PLO, ngày nghỉ, phiên rút ngắn và qua đêm. Cho đến khi có quyết định phiên bản clock policy được nghiệm thu, không tự đổi sang EXPIRED chỉ dựa trên 45 phút trôi qua; gắn trạng thái UNVERIFIED về time-based eligibility. Tăng độ mới quote không được gia hạn zone vô thời hạn; chỉ structural update có chứng cứ mới cho phép xem xét update tuổi của zone.
P15 RVOL_TOD reference = 20 prior valid comparable trading sessions at same exact session slot; not necessarily the last 20 calendar days. Wrong/missing slot => UNVERIFIED.
P14 VWAP exact = matched traded value / matched traded volume within verified session; matched_value semantics and volume parity must be verified; avg_price or typical_price*volume is a proxy, never exact.
These are INITIAL CANDIDATE THRESHOLDS, not calibrated or approved optimal. Future tests A/B/C compare M30-only vs M15 baseline vs adaptive M5, alert timeliness, false-alert rate, signal quality, latency/API calls and R:R after trading constraints. Parameter changes version P03/P16 and invalidate only relevant caches.


4. TRIỂN KHAI LOGIC TRONG PIPELINE 8 BƯỚC — TRÁNH VÒNG LẶP


Step01 DATA MANAGER: resolve symbol/source/cached latest closed TF M15,D1,H1,W1, clock, position, quota; prepare request scope and valid source digests.
Step02 DATA VALIDATOR: check calendar, price basis compatibility, confidence, matched source gap/duplicates, provider age. Weak/incomplete source => branch with evidence.
Step03 ANALYSIS ENGINE PHASE A (pre-screen): refresh/reuse M15 baseline, D1/H1 support/resistance and cached prior L3/L4 Watch Zones; produce WATCH CANDIDATE independent of current M5 so no circular dependency.
Step03 PHASE B (conditional M5): apply state machine; on ARMED fetch/reuse M5 data and compute micro indicators from closed M5 state + provisional overlay. If M5 unavailable, record quality and preserve M15-only analysis. Expensive history fetch only if cache/source capability requires; SSI MCP may require full-day 1m network request followed by client-side delta.
Step04 EXIT PROTECTION: assess position, stop/limit/settlement risk, risk override ARMED_PROTECTION if needed. If position state changes after pre-screen, reconcile M5 fetch at most once more during this run and record reentry; run must not claim Step04 COMPLETE before position evidence is final.
Step05 ZONE DETECTION: evaluate/REUSED_VALIDATED all B1..B8 and S1..S8 using finalized available features and source quality. If a zone found near boundary needs mandatory M5 and was not armed in pre-screen, perform a BOUNDED IN-RUN ESCALATION (fetch M5 if source permits, re-run affected Step03/05 processing) before committing Step05. No infinite pass loop; if missing, mark UNVERIFIED with reason. Never mark Stage05 Complete only because a cache was accessed.
Step06 SIGNAL ENGINE: separate SHORT/SWING, entry/SL/TP1/TP2/max chase/R:R/exit protection; early vs confirmed states; no short sale.
Step07 AUDIT & DECISION: 8 topics per candidate, 16-zone coverage evidence, source provider/closed bars/risk.
Step08 REPORT/HISTORY: stage, validate, commit idempotent snapshot, mark 8th step COMPLETE, then publish UI v1.0 FULL only if all mandatory gates met. Otherwise INCOMPLETE/ERROR with exact blocker.
Output indicators must distinguish RECOMPUTED, UPDATED, REUSED_VALIDATED, UNVERIFIED. Source quality may cause incomplete analysis while still reporting readable diagnostics; no fictional trading action.


5. STRICT CANDLE CLOSE & NO LOOK-AHEAD


One M5 bucket [10:10,10:15) has source minute 10:10, 10:11, 10:12 at decision time 10:12, hence OPEN_PROVISIONAL even if provisional price crosses a pivot. ARMED may show WATCH/EARLY warning, never confirmed on unclosed candle. Only when close_time<=decision_time and provider quality confirms all required trade/session records may M5 close evidence become CONFIRMED. Some UPCoM slots contain no minute record; elapsed wall clock alone does not prove completeness.
M15 confirmed structural pivot needs its required right bars that were already closed; M5 cannot retroactively turn an unconfirmed D1 pivot into confirmed. Each alert must carry event_id, source cutoff, zone revision, candle status, model/code version, quality, alert/dedup key. During replay, indicator or zone first-known time must be <= decision_at. Provisional indicator preview computed from last confirmed state and discarded/rebuilt on revision, not smoothed repeatedly as close.
Zone ID revision, source adjustment and calendar version change invalidate microstructure and prior armed state if applicable; no duplicate alert when querying the same 5-minute snapshot twice.


6. RVOL-TOD — LỌC ĐÚNG SLOT, KHÔNG ĐOÁN


Define (for a CLOSED current slot) RVOL_TOD = current verified slot volume / average verified prior N=20 volumes at same exchange, session, exact intraday time bucket, matching duration and comparable trading regime. For cumulative session RVOL vs per-slot RVOL use distinct named feature and version; do not mix.
Eligibility requires 20 valid comparable prior sessions (not counting current), same exchange, same bucket clock anchor, native/derived session policy compatible, no unresolved sparse-slot gap, volume unit consistent, confidence that 0-volume means confirmed no trades. Exclude holidays, session partial/auction mismatch, adjustments and missing source. Denominator zero or sample count <20 -> UNVERIFIED, not numeric 0; current slot open -> PREVIEW (if used) but not confirmed.
POC fixture: 20 historical slots of 100 shares and current CLOSED slot=120 => RVOL_TOD=1.20. With 19 slots, one different clock slot, sparse ambiguous slot or current open, return UNVERIFIED. Fixture synthetic; SSI MCP long intraday retention and real slot completeness still have blockers.


7. VWAP, ANCHORED VWAP & LIQUIDITY GATES


VWAP exact eligibility = sum(verified matched traded value) / sum(matched traded volume) over same verified session with provider-defined semantics; price quote avg_price field not automatically trade-weighted VWAP, OHLC typical-price approximation cannot be labeled exact. VWAP source on SSI intraday M1 has not yet been verified with matched_value semantics, so mark VWAP_VALUE_MISSING/UNVERIFIED for strict exact feature. Anchored VWAP requires causal anchor event/time and event revision, not hindsight-optimized anchor; invalidate on corporate action/late source correction.
For M5 Early trigger requiring VWAP/RVOL to assert CONFIRMED and unavailable, mark that feature UNVERIFIED and apply rule-specific gating; do not upgrade merely from price cross. Features not mandatory for an independent D1 rule should not blanket-block the entire stock if other evidence is sufficient; still report warnings.
Liquidity, ceiling/floor and trade settlement constraints are separate execution gates; no assumed executable trade simply because synthetic trigger conditions look true.


8. CACHE L1–L5 VÀ 5-PHÚT RECALL — ĐÚNG KỲ VỌNG USER


Cache applies to ALL reusable assets, not just candles: M1/W1/D1/M5/M15/M30/H1, market/index/sector/context, corporate actions, technical indicators, structure, all 16 B/S zones, strategies, audits and report history. On user recall after ~5 min:
L1 reuse confirmed historical bars/events, detect append/revise from provider; may FETCH full-day M1 if SSI MCP doesn't support server-side delta, but only recompute changed canonical bars locally.
L2 EMA/ATR/RSI/volume features reuse previous checkpoint for unchanged confirmed bars, M15 update when new input closes, M5 micro state only when armed and source permits; provisional overlay separate from closed state.
L3 structural pivots/events unaffected by revisions reuse valid results; source adjustment, calendar, market/sector changes invalidate affected paths.
L4 all 16 detector entries and SHORT/SWING decision must be evaluated or REUSED_VALIDATED; when M5 off and detector does not need it, no forced M5 fetch, but M5-dependent confirmation remains UNVERIFIED.
L5 each user query gets an audit run_id, step/zone evidence, cache counters and ERROR/INCOMPLETE if source mandatory unavailable. Do not blindly reuse prior decision as a new confirmed trade because price moved while cache remained unchanged.
No background refresh/scheduler has been created or approved. M5 'auto arm' means conditional behavior DURING A REQUESTED SCAN, not an always-on service unless user later separately authorizes a scheduler. No broker trade execution.

16. PRE-CODE STATE-MACHINE FREEZE v0.2 — DETERMINISTIC EVENTS, M5 EFFICIENCY & FAILURE SAFETY — 06/11/2026
16.1. Scope and contract invariants
M15 is mandatory baseline analysis cadence and source for early structure; M5 is adaptive OPTIONAL fetch/feature stage, never a gate that prevents all 16 B/S detector IDs being evaluated. M5 can ARM by near validated Watch Zone, verified M15 early candidate, or independently by OPEN_VERIFIED position protection. A tentative quote/open M15 can ARM fetch/preview, cannot confirm or count toward verified consecutive CLOSED M15 disarm. Every decision references one consistent as_of snapshot (bar IDs, source revisions, calendar and position snapshot), never future pivot/price.
16.2. State machine (per StateScopeKey v1.2 tại §14.3; MarketBarKey/ObservationRevision tại 05 §2). Mỗi zone/strategy có state riêng, position_scope_id là ID ổn định khi có vị thế, KHÔNG dùng position_revision/source_revision tạo state mới. calendar/aggregation/price-basis/rule/param version change => migration checkpoint có kiểm chứng, fail closed nếu không thể replay; không reset bất thường để kích hoạt lại tín hiệu.
States IDLE, ARMED, ARMED_PROTECTION, COOLDOWN, EXPIRED; separate quality_status VERIFIED|WARN|UNVERIFIED|BLOCKED and eligibility WATCH_ONLY|ACTION_ELIGIBLE|BLOCKED. Priority ORDER: source invalidation/safety quarantine > explicit position protection > verified candidate/near zone ARM > valid hysteresis DISARM > time-based cooldown/expiry (only when approved calendar policy) > IDLE. ARMED_PROTECTION persists for OPEN_VERIFIED position while risk check requires it; missing M5 yields warning/blocked confirmed micro-trigger, NEVER fake safe exit; if position not supplied, do not assume flat.
16.3. Event identity / revision / replay
UniqueBarId/M15MarketBarId = MarketBarKey (05 §2) trên TF=M15; không chứa provider_id, origin, source_revision, run_id hoặc position_revision. ProviderObservationKey + ObservationRevision chỉ ghi provenance/reconciliation. StateScopeKey từ §14.3 giữ scope ổn định. SemanticAlertKey=(StateScopeKey,M15MarketBarId,trigger_type,phase,rule_version); AlertContentRevision=(SemanticAlertKey,observation_revision_or_resolved_digest,as_of_decision_snapshot). Với cùng SemanticAlertKey và cùng chứng cứ, quét 5 phút hay retry không tạo sự kiện mới; correction hoặc nguồn thay đổi phải replay, cập nhật linked supersedes/RETRACTED nếu kết luận thay đổi, không phát lại cùng lời cảnh báo chỉ vì source_snapshot khác. Nếu không replay được/không xác minh provider parity thì UNVERIFIED, không confirmed disarm/entry. M15 farCount chỉ tăng khi có HAI MarketBarKey khác nhau và ordinal liên tiếp CLOSED+VERIFIED, không do dữ liệu khác revision.
16.4. Guard table — required event → transition → side effect
T-01 IDLE + validated near-zone OR verified M15 candidate (cùng source/price-basis/calendar, zone_revision đang hiệu lực) → ARMED/watch; tạo semantic arm event và M5 fetch scope theo bounded policy. COOLDOWN KHÔNG rearm chỉ vì giá NEAR: chỉ khi clock_policy_version đã duyệt và cooldown đủ thời gian, candidate/zone còn hợp lệ thì COOLDOWN→IDLE→ARMED; khi chính sách thời gian chưa duyệt, giữ COOLDOWN với time_rearm_eligibility=UNVERIFIED. EXPIRED không được trực tiếp → ARMED: cần structural event mới được xác minh tạo zone_revision mới và StateScopeKey mới ở IDLE, sau đó mới chạy T-01. OPEN_VERIFIED position protection theo T-02 có quyền ưu tiên, nhưng không mặc nhiên tái kích hoạt vùng BUY cũ; identical event không phát alert lần hai.
T-02 ANY + OPEN_VERIFIED position with active protection reason → ARMED_PROTECTION; position refresh takes priority even if zone far/TTL passed; Step04 checks risk with verified data and emits warning if blocked.
T-03 ARMED + new distinct calendar-adjacent VERIFIED CLOSED M15 far beyond disarmThreshold, minHold policy verified → farCount=1; stay ARMED; same M15 ID re-read => no mutation.
T-04 ARMED + second distinct VERIFIED CLOSED M15 far, ordinal adjacent to first, minHold policy verified → COOLDOWN; prevent new M5 fetch unless protection/valid rearm; all 16 B/S still evaluated.
T-05 ARMED + near zone verified CLOSED event → farCount=0; stay ARMED; if farCount interrupted by unverified CLOSED candle or missing ordinal, set verified-consecutive count=0 and preserve safety status (no forced cooldown).
T-06 ARMED/COOLDOWN + source revised/zone revised/out-of-order → REPLAY_REQUIRED checkpoint; do not add event to live counters until deterministic replay passes.
T-07 ARMED/COOLDOWN + exchange session boundary, holiday, lunch, auction or missing clock semantics → preserve state with time-based expiry eligibility UNVERIFIED, no unapproved EXPIRED transition. If unverified source makes M5 unsafe, block confirmed M5 trigger but continue independent M15/position warning.
T-08 ARMED/COOLDOWN + verified/approved time policy, valid session clock, approved zone TTL threshold, no protection/active candidate → EXPIRED with event id + clock version + elapsed trading/wall minutes. Không có clock evidence thì giữ trạng thái trước đó, ghi UNVERIFIED, không tự hết hạn. Structural invalidation VERIFIED theo PRIORITY 3 có thể EXPIRED dù TTL chưa chốt. COOLDOWN timer hết không đồng nghĩa trực tiếp ARMED; chỉ COOLDOWN→IDLE sau cooldown approved và candidate/zone còn live; T-01 mới đánh giá ARM. EXPIRED revision không được tái dùng.
T-09 ARMED + missing or stale M5 → keep M15 baseline result, output M5-dependent rule phase UNVERIFIED; no full-response false 'NO SIGNAL'.
16.5. M5 bounded fetch and alert efficiency
Step03 phase A uses cached last verified L3 zone and M15+D1/H1 evidence. On ARM, check M5 source data capability; read cached valid M5 or fetch only missing window when provider permits. If provider only supports full-day M1, coalesce requests per symbol+session+source revision and derive M5 locally, measure requests and quota, never assume every 5-min recall gets a server delta. Step05 detector requiring newly armed M5 may trigger at most one bounded in-run escalation and re-evaluation of affected DAG nodes, not an unlimited loop. If M5 source remains inadequate, output UNVERIFIED for affected phase with exact reason; rest of zones evaluate normally. Alert dedup by event identity and evidence hash; watch proximity can send early alert before confirmed but must include explicit provisional/quality label.
