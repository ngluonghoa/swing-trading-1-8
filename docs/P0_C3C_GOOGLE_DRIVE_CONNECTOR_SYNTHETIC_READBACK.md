## Why this is NOT the final Smart Cache

This is a **transport smoke**, not evidence that `encode_fixture_chunk()` from C3B or C4A weekly bar-chunks are uploaded directly. The source Google Docs export route is a connector file-ref workaround, **NOT** the authenticated Python raw Drive binary adapter, not atomic multi-file upload, not source-signed provenance, not licensed SSI/DNSE/VCBS retention or a 5-minute incremental market scanner. It proves read/write/readback for two harmless files under a known private folder, at one point in time. A single SHA manifest stored next to the chunk cannot defeat a malicious rollback of both files or stale multi-writer pointers. Missing parent metadata or owner-only evidence must fail closed.
